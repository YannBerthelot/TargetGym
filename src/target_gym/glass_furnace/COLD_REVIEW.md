# Glass furnace cold review (charter rule 13)

Read: REALITY.md, physical_env.py, physical.py, charter Amendments 1 to 3. Nothing was run; everything below is from reading. Paths are under src/target_gym/glass_furnace/.

1. The agent moves the fuel valve every 120 s; operators move setpoints. REALITY section 2 says operators set the crown and glass-bottom setpoints by hand, firing follows the crown below them, tuning takes weeks to months [S4], and MPC sets the daily firing where installed [S1, S2]. The task action is the raw valve in kg/s (physical_env.step, fuel_min 0.25 and fuel_max 0.75, both ours), applied instantly with no valve lag and no crown loop beneath it. The agent is therefore a firing controller at 2-minute cadence against lags of 1 to 16 h [S7]. Effect: closed-loop designs that no operator could run (fast feedback on the crown couple) are allowed, and the real difficulty (choosing a setpoint that holds for hours) is bypassed. Nothing else operators move is on offer either: firing profile or port split [S15] is fixed (physical.port_shares, ours), no boost [S4, S5], no waist cooling [S17], no tweel. Port shares not found in REALITY, so the hot spot can never be moved.

2. The scored constraint variable is observed almost directly. REALITY section 7: quality is read at the cold end as defect counts, unit and resolution "not found". The task gives obs[4] as the flow-weighted fining share itself, smooth, with Gaussian noise 0.005 (quality_noise, ours), 18 min late (quality_delay_h 0.3, inside the 14 to 20 min family range). An agent can regulate the exact quantity that triggers loss, with a clean measurement. Real operators have discrete scanner counts that cannot show a margin to a threshold. Effect: the central difficulty ("quality read only after forming") is largely removed, and a feedback controller on obs[4] is close to an oracle for the loss term.

3. Crown limit: REALITY section 3 says wear, a cost over the campaign, "No automatic protection found" [S13, S14], and crown wear "has no family price". physical_env.override() is a PI protection that trims the valve to keep the hottest face under 1893.15 K minus 5 K (override_margin ours). The file header admits this is a known deviation (owner, 2026-11-08). Differences: (a) kind is "protected" where the source supports "cost"; (b) the override reads the true hottest roof cell, which no operator instrument reads (the agent's crown couple sits at 36 mm depth, COUPLE_DEPTH, ours, bay 3, ours); (c) nothing is charged for time near the limit, so Amendment 3's "priced, or measured as is" is met by neither; (d) the agent sees the valve it got (obs[8] is post-override), which reveals the override's state. Effect: the limit is free. It only ever binds in a hot gas swing, since normal operation is 1550 to 1580 C [S1] against 1620 C. The score has no wear term, so the "without wearing the crown" part of the job sentence (section 6) is unscored.

4. The loss term has no source for its price and is a step function evaluated on instantaneous values. reward_terms: glass is "lost" when canal < canal_target or fining < fining_pass, charged at the current pull times lost_glass_price (600 EUR/t, "ours, no source found"; section 3 says "not found"). With the design fuel (0.45 kg/s, 22.5 MW) one step of fuel costs about 45 EUR, and one step of loss at design pull about 417 EUR, so one lost step is worth about 9 steps of fuel. Effect: a cliff, ten times steeper than the fuel signal, and its height is arbitrary. Any policy with a generous margin loses almost nothing but fuel, so the optimum depends on a number nobody sourced. Also, the loss is charged on the glass charged now (pull) judged by the canal state now, whereas that glass left the charger 12 to 68 h ago [S11, S12]; the timing is unlike a real furnace, where the bad glass was charged hours or days before the symptom.

5. The fixed fining criterion is 0.5 of the flow-weighted mean share (fining_pass, labelled ours inside Beerkens' band [S31]). A mean of 0.5 allows a large share of the flow with no fining at all; the source says bubbles leave "only when fining is complete" [S12, S31]. Shortest-path glass is 0.15 to 0.20 of mean residence [S12], so real defects come from the fast streamlines; the task judges the flux-weighted mean at the canal, which hides them. Effect: the quality zone is looser than the source and may sit far from where real defects appear. Fining only depends on temperature in the 1420 to 1500 C band (physical.fining_done) and never decays; no seeds from refiner cooling, no stones, no reboil. Not found in REALITY, so none of this can be tested.

6. The canal is held by a PI loop inside the task, not by the agent or the sources. env.step runs a refiner cooling loop with setpoint canal_target + 1 K (cooling_above, ours), gain by SIMC, capacity twice design (cooling_max, "no capacity found"), and the agent sees its output (obs[5]). REALITY [S6] has the canal pyrometer driving the tweel, with feedback suspended during a reversal; cooling by loop is not sourced (section 2 cites coolers and air only, with no loop). Effects: the canal can only be lost by under-firing (cooling cannot heat); over-firing costs fuel only, and the cooling is free (no fan or electricity cost anywhere in reward_terms); obs[3] is the loop's regulated variable so it carries little information, while obs[5] leaks the heat excess. This is a closed-loop design element (charter rule 11), not a sourced behaviour.

7. Episode and events (rules 17, 20). physical_env.reset starts from fields the caller supplies as "settled", but draws the five walks at their stationary spread and sets the starting pull and cullet from them, so the plant starts out of step with its own disturbance. The default scenario has pull_change = 0 and gas_swing = 0, i.e. a quiet normal day; the suite itself (the list of scenarios) is not in this file, only two fields. The event start is uniform on 24 to 96 h (pull_change_window_h, ours) and episodes are 5040 steps (7 days, physical.max_steps_in_episode). Sizes, ramps and notice are ours: pull ramp 2 h and notice 8 h (REALITY: "Float pull-change sizes, frequency, notice and ramp rate: not found"); gas swing ramp 6 h (ENTSOG only gives "more than 3 MJ/m3 within a day" [S20], and it is not stated whether LNG swings reach a float furnace). Effect: the bad days of section 5 (pull changes making setpoints be repeated, 8 h of cycling [S9], 20 to 50 % loss reduction by MPC [S2]) exist only if the suite sets a size. The 8 h notice with a noise-free plan is a free feedforward of a size the agent can pre-compensate.

8. Walks. walk_sd = (0.02, 0.01, 0.05, 0.10, 0.17) with 24 or 12 h memory. Gas 2 % is "four spreads" of ENTSOG's range (ours reading); pull 1 % sd as "two spreads" of the 2 % wander [S5]; cullet 5 points and blanket thickness 10 % are the owner's picks; sulfate 17 % is ours. Real gas composition is read weekly to daily [S19] and the task hides it, which agrees. But real pull wander is stepwise from the forming line, not a smooth AR(1). Effect: slow, smooth, small disturbances against hour-scale lags let a constant or slowly corrected valve do nearly as well as a tracking controller (see 12).

9. Instruments. Noise is white, one sd 0.36 K (noise_sd, "shipped crown couple's", ours for the rest) on crown, two floor readings, canal, air preheat and stack. REALITY: crown, bottom and canal "delay and accuracy: not found"; real couples are in platinum thimbles with lag, drift and a reversal-synchronous ripple. The task has no delay on couples other than the crown's conduction depth, no drift, no bias, and the reversal is averaged out (physical.py header; 25 min period, 40 s dead time in env.py) so the crown couple has no reversal ripple and the canal feedback is never suspended [S6]. The floor reading "at the melter's end" is not at the boost-burner location of [S5]. obs[6] air preheat and obs[7] stack are not in REALITY section 1 at all (no source says operators read them or how); they give the agent regenerator state, which operators may have but is unsourced. obs[10] pull planned is exact and obs[9] pull and obs[11] cullet are noise-free, which fits batch-plant load cells [S43] but not the sourced "about every 2 min" cadence being matched explicitly. The batch line is read every 30 min (batch_line_every_h) from [S23] as relayed by a reviewer, "not re-read" in REALITY; it is read as a clean metre value with no noise, where real cameras give a normalised statistic in a different furnace type.

10. Plant structure versus REALITY section 8. The model has six ports, melter, unfired stretch at full width, waist, working end: this matches [S25, S10, S18]. Missing or different: no cross-firing asymmetry or reversal side, the blanket is a 1D wedge across the full width (cover(), advance_blanket) where real batch islands cover 85 % of the island zone [S10] in patches; no glass-level loop or tweel; no waist coolers (the source says they set forward flow at 1.2 to 2.5 times the pull [S17], the task's forward flow is whatever the buoyancy flow gives and was not checked against that range in the files read); no electric or oxy boost. Residence-time spread against [S11, S12] is not tested in anything I read.

11. Numbers with no source, as the code states: eps_surface 0.8, port_shares, COUPLE_DEPTH/BAY, cooling_max, cooling_gain and ti, cooling_above, override_margin and gains, fuel range, lost_glass_price, fining_pass, quality_noise, bubble_radius at "top of the lab range", sulfate walk. Charter rule 19 allows ours inside a family range, but for lost_glass_price, fining_pass, cooling_max and the event sizes REALITY states "not found", so no range exists to be inside.

12. What lets a constant policy do well. (a) The canal is regulated by a plant loop and cannot be lost by over-firing; (b) the crown override removes the one hazard; (c) the only cost of margin is fuel, a few percent per step against a loss cliff 9 times larger, so the cheapest robust policy is a constant valve a few percent above the fining threshold at design pull; (d) walks are slow and small and the default scenario has no event; (e) the named property (variance reduction lets the setpoint drop, worth 2 to 4 % of fuel [S2, S3]) is a small effect, and obs[4] with small noise makes the sub-hour feedback easy. A tracking controller can only win those few percent, and only if a sourced event suite adds real variance. The score has no gate that separates a controller from a constant valve except the gap-closed fraction against a reference whose back-off (rule 22) is not in this file.

13. Crown wear and campaign: REALITY gives corrosion rates only at 1450 C surface for oxy-fuel [S34] and no air-fired dependence; the task rightly does not invent a price, but Amendment 3 says "measured as is" and the task neither measures nor reports time above any crown level. The cost axis the job sentence names ("without wearing the crown") therefore cannot show in results.

14. Smaller items. Fuel price of 60.5 EUR/MWh is Eurostat H2 2025 non-household EU [S21], fine for the family, applied to energy received, so gas swings do not change cost per MWh; real billing is by energy, which agrees. Cooling air power, scanner rejects, and cullet remelt cost are not charged. State vs observation: obs[8] fuel is after the override and after the valve clip, not the asked value. The initial-fields gap at reset (item 7) means the first hours are a transient the agent did not cause, charged against it.
