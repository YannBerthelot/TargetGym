    Multi-agent formation¶

    The formation task puts a learner in every seat of a three-ship formation. The lead flies a patrol route: an altitude, and a heading that turns onto a new rate every minute. Two wingmen hold their slots in a V behind it, one on each side. All three share one cost, so the lead flying its route well is worth as much as the wingmen keeping station.

    It is the patrol with the scripted lead replaced by a learner. If you want a single follower behind a lead you do not control, use patrol instead.

    Two views of one task¶

    view use it for actions observations reward
    Formation single-agent algorithms (one controller flies every aircraft), the baselines and the protocol one array of 9 one array of 74 one team reward
    FormationMARL multi-agent algorithms (one policy per aircraft, or shared parameters) a dict, 3 per agent a dict, per agent the team reward, given to every agent

    Both run the same step, so a team scores the same in either view. The registered task is Formation, and its scored episode is 200 steps of 1 s:

    import jax
import target_gym as tg

env, params = tg.make("formation")   # the scored 200-step episode

obs, state = env.reset_env(jax.random.PRNGKey(0), params)   # obs.shape == (74,)
action = env.action_space(params).sample(jax.random.PRNGKey(1))   # (9,)
obs, state, reward, terminated, truncated, info = env.step(
    jax.random.PRNGKey(2), state, action, params
)

    The multi-agent view follows the JaxMARL MultiAgentEnv conventions: agents are "lead", "wingman_0" and "wingman_1", step resets every agent when dones["__all__"] is true, and everything is jit, vmap and scan compatible.

    import jax
import target_gym as tg

env = tg.FormationMARL()
_, params = tg.make("formation")
key = jax.random.PRNGKey(0)

obs, state = env.reset(key, params)   # {"lead": (22,), "wingman_0": (26,), "wingman_1": (26,)}
actions = {a: env.action_space(a).sample(key) for a in env.agents}
obs, state, rewards, dones, info = env.step(key, state, actions, params)
# rewards: the same team reward for every agent
# info["costs"]: each aircraft's own cost this step

    What each aircraft controls and sees¶

    Every aircraft acts with [power, stick, aileron], each in [-1, 1]. In the joint view the lead's three come first, then each wingman's.

    seat observation what it contains
    lead 22 values its own flight state (15 values, the 3D heading task's), whose target is the heading the route commands this step; the route's current turn rate; each wingman's slot error (behind, right, up, in the lead's frame)
    each wingman 26 values the patrol's observation: its own flight state, the lead's position and velocity relative to it, its slot, and its slot error

    A wingman sees exactly what the patrol's follower sees, so a policy trained on patrol can fly a wingman seat unchanged. The wingmen do not see each other; the V puts them at least 120 m apart, and only a wingman far out of its slot comes near the other.

    The joint observation is the lead's 22 values followed by each wingman's 26. The tracked values are the lead's altitude (index 2) and each wingman's slot error (indices 46 and 72).

    The reward¶

    One team cost, charged every step and given to every agent:

    • the lead's altitude error, in units of 1 m (an altimeter's resolution), squared;
    • the lead's heading error against its route, in units of 0.5 deg, squared;
    • each wingman's distance from its slot, in units of 3 m (relative GPS), squared;
    • each wingman's heading difference from the lead, in units of 0.5 deg, squared.

    The reward is minus their sum, the same contract as every TargetGym task (see Reward shaping). In the multi-agent view info["costs"] splits it by aircraft, for credit assignment: the costs add up to minus the reward on every step without a trip.

    Trips and restarts¶

    The formation trips when any aircraft leaves the altitude band, any two aircraft come within 25 m of each other, or a wingman ends up 1500 m from its slot. The step that trips is charged the trip cost and sets info["tripped"] and info["trip_cost"], and the whole formation restarts on the same episode clock. The episode does not end.

    For episodic training, set continuing=False in the params: a trip then ends the episode, and dones["__all__"] is true in the multi-agent view.

    Baselines¶

    Two baselines ship, recorded on the Baseline results page.

    • PID team (tg.make_expert("formation").host(), or .batched() for JAX): the lead seat flies the route as a formation lead does, on the 3D heading task's autopilot at a set power, and each wingman seat flies the patrol's formation hold. On held-out seeds the lead holds its altitude within 1.6 m and the wingmen their slots within 2.7 to 7.7 m RMS, except one wingman that rejoins late from a high spawn (formation's PHYSICS.md, D3).
    • Oracle (make_oracle(name)): each seat flies a law, the patrol's lead autopilot or the patrol's twin-autopilot law, and one planner adds a correction to every seat over a 30-step horizon, on the true team cost. It holds the lead to under 1 m and each slot to about 0.3 m. Like every oracle in the suite it reads the true state rather than the observations, and it can coordinate the team only as far as its horizon reaches.

    Other formation sizes¶

    Formation(num_wingmen=k) and FormationMARL(num_wingmen=k) take 1 to 4 wingmen. The lead observes 16 + 3k values and each wingman 26; the joint action has 3(k + 1). Wingmen alternate sides, right first, and each pair sits one rank further back and out. Only the two-wingman formation is scored. For another size, build the controllers with the size:

    from target_gym import Formation
from target_gym.formation.experts import (
    make_formation_mpc,
    make_formation_pid_controller,
)

env = Formation(num_wingmen=4)
pid = make_formation_pid_controller(num_wingmen=4).host()
oracle = make_formation_mpc(env, env.default_params)
